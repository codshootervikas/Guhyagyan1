✧ Key Topics

01
Philosophy & Nature of Harinama

Understanding the philosophy, power, and nature of the Holy Name (Harinama).

02
Cleansing the Heart Through Sankirtana

Removing impurities from the heart through sincere Harinama-sankirtana (congregational chanting).

03
Deep Connection in Humility

Establishing a deep and lasting connection through constant chanting and practice, performed with humility.
